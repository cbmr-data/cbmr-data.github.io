.. _p_usage_access_applying:

##############################
 Applying for access to Esrum
##############################

The following describes the process for getting access to Esrum, for
:ref:`employees at CBMR <s_usage_access_employees>`, as well as for
:ref:`external researcher, and other people not employed at CBMR
<s_usage_access_guests>`. Do not hesitate to :ref:`contact us
<p_contact>`, if you have any questions regarding this process.

.. tip::

    If you need access to a group that is *not* controlled by the Data
    Analytics Platform, or if you need to give another person access to
    a group that *you* control, then please see the
    :ref:`p_identity_management_system`.

.. _s_usage_access_employees:

******************************
 Access for employees at CBMR
******************************

Access to the Esrum HPC cluster and a number of related services,
projects, and datasets are managed by the Data Analytics Platform. To
get access,

1. Retrieve the IDs of your scientific projects from the CBMR Project
   Manager (e.g. ``CBMR-ABCD12``):

   - You need to be connected to the VPN to access the CBMR Project
     manager.
   - If you have previously registered your scientific project(s), then
     you can look up the project IDs via the `Search Project`_ tab in
     the `Project Manager`_.
   - If you haven't registered the scientific projects that you intend
     to work on using Esrum, then visit the `Project Manager`_ and
     register your project as shown.

2. Email us at `SUND-CBMR-Esrum
   <mailto:cbmr-data-analytics@sund.ku.dk>`_, and include your UCPH ID
   and the IDs of your scientific project(s) from the `Project
   Manager`_.
3. (Optional) To get access to human cohort data managed by the Data
   Analytics Platform, follow the instructions on the
   :ref:`p_human_cohorts` page. You must have applied for access to
   Esrum before doing so.

Once your application for access to Esrum has been approved, you will
receive a welcome email from the Data Analytics Platform with further
information about using the cluster, as well as an automated email from
``NoReply@ku.dk`` with the title ``Changes to your Identity were
processed``, which indicates that your account has been updated.

.. _s_usage_access_guests:

*****************************************
 Access for people NOT employeed at CBMR
*****************************************

Access to Esrum for external researchers, students, guests, and other
people collaborating with scientists at CBMR, *must* be requested by an
existing employee at CBMR. Please follow these instructions when
applying for access to Esrum for a collaborator:

1. Your guests must be registered with the `CBMR reception
   <mailto:CBMR-Reception@sund.ku.dk>`_, and your group leader at CBMR
   must fill out the `External Guest Form`_, if you have not already
   done so. As part of this process, your collaborator will receive a
   UCPH account (i.e. a ``abc123`` account).
2. If your collaborator needs access to sensitive data, such as human
   data or data stored on the :ref:`S-drive <p_network_drives>`, then
   they will need to sign a Data Processing Agreement. The reception
   will guide you through this process.
3. You or your collaborator must register their scientific projects in
   the CBMR `Project Manager`_ (e.g. ``CBMR-ABCD12``), if you/they have
   not already done so. Note that you need to be connected to the UCPH
   VPN to access the Project manager.

   - If they have previously registered their scientific project(s),
     then you can look up the project IDs via the `Search Project`_ tab
     in the `Project Manager`_.
   - If they haven't registered the scientific projects that they intend
     to work on using Esrum, then visit the `Project Manager`_ and
     register their projects as shown.

4. Email us at `SUND-CBMR-Esrum
   <mailto:cbmr-data-analytics@sund.ku.dk>`_, and include your
   collaborator's UCPH ID and the IDs of their scientific project(s)
   from the `Project Manager`_.
5. (Optional) Once your collaborator has applied for access to Esrum,
   they may also apply access to human cohort data managed by the Data
   Analytics Platform. See the :ref:`p_human_cohorts` page for more
   information.

The Data Analytics platform will verify that the appropriate agreements
are in place, and then grant your collaborator access to Esrum. As part
of the onboarding, they will receive a welcome email to their UCPH email
address with further information about using the cluster. Additionally,
they will receive an automated email from ``NoReply@ku.dk`` with the
title ``Changes to your Identity were processed``, which indicates that
their account has been updated.

.. attention::

    Guests must ensure that they receive and read email sent to their
    UCPH email-address, for example by forwarding the emails to their
    primary email address. Failure to read and respond to emails sent by
    the Data Analytics Platform will result in access to Esrum being
    revoked.
